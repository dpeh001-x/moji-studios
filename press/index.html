Studio boilerplate

Moji Studios is an indie game studio founded in 2026 and dedicated to the early-2000s gaming era — hand-pixeled art, cozy retro vibes, and chibi-forward character design. The studio is half game-maker, half digital design agency. Its main game, MojiWorld, is a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure launching first as a single-player game on Steam, expected July–August 2026, with a full online MMORPG to follow after launch. Moji Studios also develops Bitmon, a creature-breeding RPG with 240+ hand-pixeled creatures, and runs Chubby Bird, a free browser arcade game that is live now. The studio's mascot is Guguma, a chick named after sweet potatoes. More at https://moji-studios.com and on Instagram @mojistudios.official.

Fact sheet: the games

MojiWorld — main game

A hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure, launching first as a single-player game on Steam, expected July–August 2026 (announced June 2026 as "1–2 months out"). The full online MMORPG follows after launch. Built around 12 classes with 8 continents planned. Platform: PC via Steam (only confirmed platform). Price: to be announced. Full details: MojiWorld game page.

Bitmon — in development

A creature-breeding RPG with 240+ hand-pixeled creatures. Each Bitmon has a personality, a lineage, and a genetics system; online trading is part of the design. Status (2026): in development — "stay tuned for updates." No platform, price, or release date has been announced. Full details: Bitmon game page.

Chubby Bird — live now

A free casual arcade browser game starring the Moji chick — one-button, tap-to-flap, with a leaderboard. No install needed; playable right now at moji-studios.com/chubbybird/. Full details: Chubby Bird game page.
