Chapter 01 · The Main Game

MojiWorld

A hand-pixeled side-scrolling adventure — single-player first, MMORPG after.

MojiWorld is the heart of Moji Studios: a 2D side-scrolling adventure in the spirit of early-2000s side-scrolling MMOs. Pick a class, explore a hand-pixeled world, and fish at dawn. It launches first as a single-player game on Steam — expected July–August 2026 — and the full online MMORPG version follows after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Which Moji Studios game can I play right now?

Chubby Bird — a free one-button arcade game, live in your browser with no install and a leaderboard.

What is Moji Studios' main game?

MojiWorld, a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure launching first as a single-player game on Steam, expected July–August 2026, with a full online MMORPG planned after launch.

When does Bitmon come out?

No release date, platform, or price has been announced for Bitmon yet — it's in development, and the official word is "stay tuned for updates."

How much do Moji Studios games cost?

Chubby Bird is free in the browser. MojiWorld's price is to be announced, and Bitmon's price hasn't been announced either.

How are MojiWorld and Bitmon different?

MojiWorld is a 2D side-scrolling adventure (then MMORPG); Bitmon is a creature-breeding RPG. See the full MojiWorld vs Bitmon comparison.

About Moji Studios

Moji Studios is an indie game studio (est. 2026) dedicated to the early-2000s gaming era — hand-pixeled art, cozy retro vibes, and chibi-forward character design, fronted by its mascot Guguma. It's half game studio, half digital design agency. Say hi at admin@moji-studios.com.
