MojiWorld: single-player adventure first, MMORPG later

By the Moji Studios team Updated June 13, 2026 Status: In development — Steam launch upcoming
⚡ Quick answer

MojiWorld is a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure from Moji Studios, launching first as a single-player game on Steam — expected July–August 2026. The full online MMORPG version, with guilds and multiplayer raids, follows after launch. It features 12 classes and 8 planned continents, in the spirit of early-2000s side-scrolling MMOs.

What is MojiWorld?

MojiWorld is the main game from Moji Studios, an indie studio dedicated to recreating the feel of early-2000s games. It's a 2D side-scrolling world in the spirit of the classic side-scrolling MMOs of that era: pick a class, explore a hand-pixeled world, fish at dawn, and — once the online version arrives — join a guild and raid at dusk.

Every tile of the world is hand-pixeled. The studio is building the first continent now, out of 8 planned, with 12 playable classes.

Why single-player first?

The short version

Rather than holding the whole world back until the MMO infrastructure is ready, Moji Studios is shipping MojiWorld's world as a complete single-player adventure first. You get to play the game in July–August 2026 instead of waiting; the studio gets real players exploring the continent while the online systems are finished. The full MMORPG follows after launch.

This makes MojiWorld the studio's first Steam release — following Chubby Bird, the free browser game already live today.

Release window & platforms

As of June 13, 2026, Moji Studios has announced MojiWorld will be up on Steam as a single-player game within 1–2 months — roughly July–August 2026. The exact date and price haven't been announced yet. PC via Steam is the only confirmed platform.

About Moji Studios

Moji Studios is an indie game studio (est. 2026) dedicated to the early-2000s gaming era — hand-pixeled art, cozy retro vibes, and chibi-forward character design. Alongside MojiWorld, the studio runs Chubby Bird (a free browser arcade game, live now) and is developing Bitmon, a creature-breeding RPG. It's also half digital design agency.
