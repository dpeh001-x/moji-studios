MojiWorld vs Bitmon: what's the difference?

By the Moji Studios team Updated June 13, 2026 Type: Side-by-side comparison
⚡ Quick answer

MojiWorld and Bitmon are two different upcoming games from Moji Studios — not the same game. MojiWorld is the studio's main game: a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure launching first as a single-player title on Steam, expected July–August 2026, with a full online MMORPG to follow. Bitmon is a creature-breeding RPG with 240+ hand-pixeled creatures, still in development with no release date announced.

MojiWorld in one paragraph

MojiWorld is the heart of Moji Studios — a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure in the spirit of early-2000s side-scrolling MMOs. It launches first as a single-player game on Steam, expected July–August 2026, and the full online MMORPG version follows after launch. It's built around 12 classes and 8 planned continents. Price is to be announced; PC via Steam is the only confirmed platform. Watch the official teaser.

Bitmon in one paragraph

Bitmon is a creature-breeding RPG where you raise, breed, and battle 240+ hand-pixeled creatures. Every Bitmon has a personality, a lineage, and a genetics system, and you can trade online. It's in development at Moji Studios with no release date, platform, or price announced — the official word is "stay tuned for updates." Two trailers are out: a pixel trailer and a 3D trailer.

So which should you watch?

The verdict

Want an adventure you can play soon? Watch MojiWorld — it's the studio's main game and the one with an actual window: single-player on Steam, expected July–August 2026, with the MMORPG to follow. Want creature collecting, breeding, and trading? Watch Bitmon — just know it's further out, with no release date announced yet. And if you can't wait for either, Chubby Bird is free in your browser right now.

Frequently asked questions

Are MojiWorld and Bitmon the same game?

No. They're two separate games from the same indie studio, Moji Studios. MojiWorld is a 2D side-scrolling adventure; Bitmon is a creature-breeding RPG.

Which comes first, MojiWorld or Bitmon?

MojiWorld. It launches first as a single-player game on Steam, expected July–August 2026. Bitmon is still in development with no release date announced.

Is Bitmon part of MojiWorld? Will Bitmon creatures appear in MojiWorld?

Nothing like that has been announced. Right now they're presented as two separate games, each with its own trailers and development track.

What platforms are the two games on?

MojiWorld launches on PC via Steam. Bitmon's platform hasn't been announced yet.

How much will they cost?

Pricing hasn't been announced for either game. Follow Moji Studios on Instagram or email the studio for announcements.
