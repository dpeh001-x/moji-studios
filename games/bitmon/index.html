What is Bitmon?

Bitmon is a creature-breeding RPG in development at Moji Studios, an indie studio (est. 2026) dedicated to the early-2000s gaming era. The pitch is simple: raise, breed, and battle 240+ hand-pixeled creatures — trade online, train at home, and watch your party grow across generations.

Like everything from the studio, the creatures are hand-pixeled in an early-2000s retro style. Bitmon sits alongside MojiWorld (the studio's main game, headed to Steam) and Chubby Bird (a free browser game you can play right now).

The genetics & breeding system

What sets Bitmon apart from a straight creature collector is the breeding layer. Three things are confirmed about how it works:

  • Every Bitmon has a personality — they're individuals, not interchangeable copies of a species.
  • Every Bitmon has a lineage — a genetics system ties each creature to its parents, and your party grows across generations as you breed.
  • Online trading — trading creatures with other players is a planned feature.
The short version

Bitmon is the "raise and breed" half of the Moji Studios lineup: 240+ hand-pixeled species, each with a personality, a lineage, and a future. Deeper mechanical details — battle systems, stats, how genetics combine — haven't been detailed publicly yet.

The studio has shared two trailers so far: a pixel trailer (above, bitmon-promo.mp4) and a 3D trailer.

Release status: no date yet — here's the honest answer

As of June 13, 2026, Bitmon has no announced release date, no announced platform, and no announced price. The game is listed as in development for 2026, and the studio's official status is simply "stay tuned for updates." We'd rather say that plainly than invent a window.
