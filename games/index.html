Games by Moji Studios

By the Moji Studios team Updated June 13, 2026 Lineup: 3 games — 1 playable right now
⚡ Quick answer

Moji Studios currently has three games: Chubby Bird, a free casual arcade game live now in your browser; MojiWorld, the studio's main game — a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure launching first as a single-player game on Steam, expected July–August 2026; and Bitmon, a creature-breeding RPG in development with 240+ hand-pixeled creatures and no announced release date yet.

MojiWorld — the main game

MojiWorld is the heart of Moji Studios: a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure in the spirit of early-2000s side-scrolling MMOs, built around 12 classes and 8 planned continents. It launches first as a single-player game on Steam — expected July–August 2026 — and the full online MMORPG version follows after launch. PC via Steam is the only confirmed platform; price is to be announced.

Read the full MojiWorld page →

Bitmon — creature-breeding RPG

Bitmon is a creature-breeding RPG in development, where you raise, breed, and battle 240+ hand-pixeled creatures. Every Bitmon has a personality and a lineage, backed by a genetics system, and you can trade online. No platform, price, or release date has been announced — the official word is "stay tuned for updates."

Read the full Bitmon page →

Chubby Bird — play free right now

Chubby Bird is the one you can play today: a free casual arcade game that runs in your browser with no install. One button, tap to flap, starring the Moji chick — and a leaderboard to climb. It's the studio's only live release so far.

Play Chubby Bird now → or read the Chubby Bird page →

About Moji Studios

Moji Studios is an indie game studio (est. 2026) dedicated to the early-2000s gaming era — hand-pixeled art, cozy retro vibes, and chibi-forward character design, fronted by its mascot Guguma. It's half game studio, half digital design agency. Say hi at admin@moji-studios.com.
